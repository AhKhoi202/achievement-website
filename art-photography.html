ART & PHOTOGRAPHY

1. Drawing My Way Through Time

I initially drew digital pictures when my dad bought a new tablet for me to study. During that time, I really engaged in digital comics, which inspired most of my pictures. From easy motif lessons and HUTA chan art tutorials, I learned how to draw anime-style and Realistic styles; from Miko and Shin-Chan, I learned how to make manga; From old books my mom got from an old bookstore, I became fascinated with proportion in separating frames and humans' scale. In my very first gallery, I have a lot of photos started by my emotions, and some of them started from my favourite motif and theme of comics. Since I began drawing in 2019, I first used PenUp to illustrate facial features of humans, like the nose, eyes, and mouth. Following up, I read through all the books about drawing from my mom and practised them during my spare time. In some ways, PenUp isn't too good at painting, so when I saw my favorite artist advertised on Ibis Paint, I tried it out and kept using it until now. Because of changing devices without proper sync, I did lose masterpieces of my youth, which can somehow show puberty's inner thoughts that I used to be unaware of. Until now, I'm still an amateur at drawing with solid purposes of joy and spreading meaning to others.
